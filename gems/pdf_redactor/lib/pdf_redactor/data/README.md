# PDFRedactor data

Adobe's data, unmodified:

- `glyphlist.txt` and `zapfdingbats.txt`: the Adobe Glyph List and the ITC
  Zapf Dingbats Glyph List, from
  https://github.com/adobe-type-tools/agl-aglfn. They are under the BSD
  3-Clause licence in each file's header.
- `afm/`: the metrics of the 14 standard PDF fonts. They may be copied and
  distributed with their copyright notices, and only with `MustRead.html`.
